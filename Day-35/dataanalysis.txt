1. What is Data Analysis?

Data Analysis is the process of collecting, cleaning, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to find useful information,
patterns, and insights.

Formula:
Raw Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Insights → Decision Making

2. Why Do We Need Data Analysis?
Data analysis is important because raw data alone is difficult to understand and use.

Main purposes:
Better Decision Making
Helps organizations make decisions based on data instead of guesses.
Identify Patterns and Trends - Helps find changes and trends in data.
Example: Sales increase every weekend.
Find Problems - Helps identify errors, unusual activities, or poor performance.
Understand Customers - Helps businesses understand customer behavior, preferences, and needs.
Improve Performance - Shows which areas are performing well and which need improvement.
Reduce Costs - Helps identify unnecessary expenses and inefficient processes.
Increase Profit - Helps companies identify profitable products, customers, and strategies.
Predict Future Outcomes - Historical data can be used to predict future trends.
Example: Predicting next month's sales.

Types of Data Analysis – Simple Definitions

There are 4 main types of data analysis:

1. Descriptive Analysis - It tells us what happened in the past or what is happening now.
Example: Sales were ₹50,000 last month.

2. Diagnostic Analysis - It tells us why something happened.
Example: Sales decreased because fewer customers purchased products.

3. Predictive Analysis - It tells us what is likely to happen in the future.
Example: Sales may increase next month.

4. Prescriptive Analysis - It tells us what action we should take.
Example: We should increase advertising to improve sales.
